# Week 1 Part C — Why My Current Major Matters for AI

## Why Math Matters for AI

Math is important for AI because many AI systems rely on mathematical ideas such as linear algebra, calculus, probability, and statistics.

For example:
- **Linear Algebra** helps represent data as vectors and matrices, which are used heavily in neural networks.
- **Calculus** helps explain how models learn through gradients and optimization.
- **Probability and Statistics** help with prediction, uncertainty, and model evaluation.

My understanding:
Math is not separate from AI. It is one of the foundations that makes AI possible.

---

## Why Data Science Is Closely Connected to Machine Learning

Data Science is closely connected to Machine Learning because machine learning depends heavily on data.

Before a model can learn, people often need to:
- collect data
- clean data
- organize data
- explore data
- analyze data

These are all common tasks in Data Science.

For example:
- If I want to predict house prices, I first need a dataset with features such as size, location, and number of rooms.
- If the dataset has missing values or messy columns, I need to clean it before using machine learning.
- If I want to understand which features matter most, I may explore the dataset first with statistics and visualizations.

My understanding:
Data Science and Machine Learning are very close because both work with data, and good machine learning usually starts with good data work.

---

## Why My Current Studies Are Foundational, Not Outdated

What I am studying now is not behind AI. It is part of the foundation I need in order to understand AI more deeply.

For example:
- Learning **programming** helps me use tools, write code, and work with datasets.
- Learning **math** helps me understand how models work instead of only memorizing terms.
- Learning **data analysis** helps me understand how to inspect and interpret data before building models.

Some people may jump directly into advanced AI terms such as transformers, agents, or RAG, but without strong foundations, it is harder to really understand them.

My understanding:
My current studies are meaningful for AI because they are building the skills I will need later.

---

## Final Reflection

AI is built on math, data, and programming.  
That means my current major is not far away from AI at all.  
Instead, it is helping me build the right foundation step by step.
